NR International Export: coconut, copra and agricultural products from Karnataka

NR International Export is a partnership registered in Hassan district, Karnataka, India. It supplies coconuts, copra, coconut oil, coconut shell, ginger, ragi (finger millet), maize and tomatoes to importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, food processors and institutional buyers, for both global export and domestic supply within India. Sourcing is focused on the Channarayapatna and Tiptur region, a coconut-growing area of Karnataka.
